# PSS-WebTest study-design freeze record v1.0

Freeze date: 2026-09-14

Status: human-confirmed design; experiment execution paused; confirmatory
authorization false.

## Frozen now

- Confirmatory core: WebArena-Verified, VisualWebArena, and the Autonomous
  Tester Agent Benchmark.
- Conditional extension: WorkArena++ only after its pre-registered environment,
  reset, evaluator, credential, and three-paradigm feasibility gate.
- Existing BookStack, Indico, Juice Shop, Invoice Ninja, and PrestaShop records
  are pilot/engineering evidence only.
- Traditional post-freeze adaptation failures remain in the
  deployment-effectiveness denominator.
- Pure-visual control flow may use only screenshot-derived observations,
  cursor/action state, explicit action errors, and declared budgets.
- Eligible tasks are screened outcome-blind and frozen before any arm runs.
- Traditional authors and reviewers remain blind to evaluator internals and
  CUA/Hybrid outcomes.
- Protocol changes are versioned; old and new protocol strata are never pooled.

## Not frozen yet

- exact benchmark release/image/license digests;
- included and excluded official task IDs;
- task-characteristic annotations and agreement statistics;
- compliant runner and run-record implementation hashes;
- framework/model configurations and prompts;
- repetition count, retry rules, and statistical analysis hash;
- confirmatory collection authorization.

No item in the second list may be inferred from previous local pilots. Each
requires its own evidence gate and future freeze record.
