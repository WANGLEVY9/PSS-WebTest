# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want PSS-WebTest to be a welcoming, evidence-oriented project for researchers, engineers, and practitioners. Participants should be able to contribute without harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or retaliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific, especially when challenging a result or design choice.
- Assume good intent while asking for reproducible evidence.
- Keep discussions focused on the code, protocol, data boundary, and research question.
- Protect credentials, personal data, and private research material.
- Acknowledge uncertainty instead of filling evidence gaps with speculation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, unwanted contact, deliberate credential exposure, and knowingly misrepresenting experimental evidence are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact route in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, request a correction, restrict participation, or close a discussion when necessary to protect participants and the integrity of the project.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and project-related interactions. It is adapted for this repository from the principles of the Contributor Covenant.
